Our Story

FROM CYBERSECURITY TO CREATIVE TECH

StudioFX started with a simple belief: businesses deserve digital partners who actually deliver. Founded in Bradenton, Florida, we've spent the last decade proving that great design and cutting-edge technology aren't mutually exclusive — and that deadlines aren't suggestions.

Our founder, John Daly, brings decades of experience spanning cybersecurity and web design. That background shapes everything we do — from bulletproof hosting configurations to conversion-focused interfaces. When you work with StudioFX, you're getting enterprise-level thinking applied to businesses of any size.

Today, we've expanded into Voice AI and automation, helping clients capture leads around the clock without adding headcount. Same obsession with results. Same commitment to actually picking up the phone when you call.
